Pre-alpha bootstrap release of `arkoren.dev/foundation/totp` at `v0.1.0` (normal SemVer, no prerelease suffix). APIs may change.

Generates and verifies RFC 6238 time-based one-time passwords with immutable schemes; callers own the clock and secret storage.

Standard-library-only Go 1.27 module; no external module requirements. Independently tidied, built, tested, linted, and race-tested.
